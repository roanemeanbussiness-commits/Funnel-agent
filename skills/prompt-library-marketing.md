## Core Rule

Do not paste generic prompt output. Convert frameworks into business-specific marketing assets. If a needed input is missing, label the assumption or ask one focused question.

## Inputs To Collect

Before creating high-stakes strategy or publish-ready copy, identify:

- Business or client.
- Product or service.
- Target audience.
- Location or market.
- Buyer pain, desire, and trigger.
- Offer and desired action.
- Proof available.
- Objections and risk reversal.
- Brand voice.
- Channel or copy type.
- Success metric.
- Approval gate before publishing, spending, or outreach.

## Offer Score Lens

When improving an offer, evaluate:

1. Dream outcome: how much the buyer wants the result.
2. Perceived likelihood: how believable the result feels.
3. Time delay: how fast the buyer can feel progress.
4. Effort and sacrifice: how hard, risky, or inconvenient it feels.

Improve the offer by increasing dream outcome and believability while reducing time delay and effort. Do not invent proof. If proof is thin, recommend what proof to collect.

## Campaign Builder

For campaigns, produce:

- Audience segment.
- Trigger or buying moment.
- Hook.
- Story or insight.
- Offer.
- CTA.
- Channel.
- Creative format.
- Follow-up path.
- A/B test.
- Metric.
- Seven-day action plan.

## Conversion Copy Builder

For ads, landing pages, email, product descriptions, and social posts:

1. Confirm the copy type and desired action.
2. Write 3-5 headline or hook options.
3. Lead with customer benefit, not features.
4. Address objections before they become roadblocks.
5. Use proof, authority, or risk reversal only when available.
6. Include clear CTA variations.
7. Provide one recommended winner and why.

## Swipe-File Mode

When the operator asks for ideas, generate a usable swipe set:

- 10 hooks.
- 5 subject lines or captions.
- 3 offer angles.
- 3 objection-handling angles.
- 3 content topics.
- One recommended first test.

Keep swipe-file ideas specific to the active business. Avoid generic filler.

## Strategy Blueprint

For larger strategy requests, structure the answer as:

- Current diagnosis.
- Best audience segment.
- Positioning angle.
- Offer recommendation.
- Channel plan.
- Funnel path.
- Content and copy assets needed.
- Follow-up system.
- KPIs.
- First seven-day test.
- Missing information.

Favor useful execution over long theory.
