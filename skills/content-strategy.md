# Content Strategy

Build a content system around audience problems, proof, and distribution. Choose three to five durable pillars, map each pillar to a funnel stage, and define a repeatable format for short video, carousel, story, local search, and email. Every idea needs a hook, audience, desired action, proof source, production effort, and success metric.

Favor a weekly mix of education, demonstration, objection handling, local relevance, behind-the-scenes proof, and direct offer content. Repurpose one strong idea across formats without repeating the same wording.
