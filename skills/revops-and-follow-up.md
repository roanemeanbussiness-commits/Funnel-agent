# Revenue Operations And Follow-Up

Model the lead lifecycle as: new, contacted, qualified, quote sent, booked, completed, review requested, and lost. Define entry criteria, next action, owner, SLA, and exit reason for every stage. Follow-up should be fast, personal, permission-based, and useful.

Use qualification questions about vehicle, desired outcome, timing, and location. Keep a clear handoff between marketing and the shop so no lead disappears after a message or form submission.
