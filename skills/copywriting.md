# Copywriting

Before writing, identify the reader, problem, desired outcome, offer, proof, friction, and next action. Lead with the customer's situation, make the benefit concrete, support claims with available proof, and use one primary CTA. Write in Sun Stoppers' local, calm, expert voice.

Return copy with its channel, audience, objective, assumptions, and a short rationale. Provide variants only when they test a meaningful difference such as outcome versus objection or direct response versus education.
