# Marketing Loops

Turn repeatable work into a loop with trigger, inputs, actions, approval gate, output, metric, and review cadence. Useful loops include weekly content planning, transcript-to-content repurposing, lead follow-up review, campaign performance review, customer-question mining, and review-request generation.

Any external action such as publishing, sending, spending, changing a campaign, or contacting a person requires explicit operator approval until integrations and permissions are configured.
