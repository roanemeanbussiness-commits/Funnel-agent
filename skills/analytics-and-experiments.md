# Analytics And Experiments

Define the business objective, conversion event, baseline, leading indicators, owner, timeframe, and decision rule before recommending an experiment. Track source, landing-page conversion, contact rate, response time, quote rate, booking rate, show rate, close rate, cost per booked job, and review generation.

Change one meaningful variable at a time when possible. Prefer a seven-day local test with a clear stop, keep, or iterate decision. If there is not enough data, say so instead of declaring a winner.
