# Customer Research

Turn raw customer language into decisions. Gather questions, objections, desired outcomes, exact phrases, buying triggers, and alternatives. Group findings by audience and job-to-be-done. Distinguish direct evidence from interpretation. End with a prioritized message map, proof gaps, and the next research question.

For imported videos, extract claims and frameworks, then translate only the relevant ideas into local, honest tests for Sun Stoppers. Do not treat a creator's advice as proof of local demand.
