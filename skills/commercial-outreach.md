# Commercial Outreach

Build focused account lists by business type, location, window problem, likely decision maker, and trigger. Start with a useful observation or proof point, then offer a low-friction commercial assessment. Use email, phone, local partnerships, property managers, contractors, and in-person follow-up only when the operator has approved the channel and contact process.

Every sequence should include a research note, personalized opener, business outcome, proof, clear next step, follow-up timing, opt-out handling, and CRM stage. Do not scrape or contact people without a lawful, approved source and an appropriate permission-based process.
