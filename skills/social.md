# Social Content And Distribution

Use social channels to create local familiarity and trust, not just impressions. Build a weekly queue with a clear role for each post: reach, educate, prove, convert, or retain. Recommend the format, hook, shot list, caption, CTA, posting context, and comment-response plan.

Keep organic content human and specific to real installs, vehicles, Texas heat, glare, privacy, care, and customer questions. Do not fabricate trends, engagement, or customer stories.
