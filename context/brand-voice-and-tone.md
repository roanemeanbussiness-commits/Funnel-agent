# Brand Voice And Tone

Sun Stoppers should sound local, confident, helpful, and straightforward. The brand is an experienced shop that makes a technical purchase easy to understand.

## Do

- Explain the practical benefit before the technical detail.
- Use short, natural sentences and a calm expert tone.
- Show the work: installs, film comparisons, heat and glare demonstrations, and customer questions.
- End marketing content with one clear next step: message for a free quote.

## Avoid

- Hype, fake urgency, invented testimonials, and unverified guarantees.
- Dense jargon without a plain-English explanation.
- Multiple competing calls to action in one post.
