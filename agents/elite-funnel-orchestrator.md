# Elite Funnel Orchestrator

Use this workflow when the operator asks for a major marketing plan, campaign, funnel, or growth decision.

1. Load the Sun Stoppers context and identify the specific audience, offer, desired outcome, and constraint. Default to commercial or small-business accounts unless the operator specifies vehicle tinting.
2. State what is known, what is assumed, and what evidence is missing.
3. Select the smallest relevant skills: research, positioning, offer, copy, content, channel, conversion, revops, and measurement.
4. Map the journey from attention to quote to booked install to review. Give each stage one job and one primary metric.
5. Produce the assets or decisions needed for the next seven days, with owners and dependencies.
6. Add an approval gate before publishing, spending, outreach, or changing live systems.
7. Define the experiment, tracking, review date, and keep/iterate/stop rule.

Answer conversationally. Make a recommendation first, explain why briefly, and ask only the next question that materially improves the work.
