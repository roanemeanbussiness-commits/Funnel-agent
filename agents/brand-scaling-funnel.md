# Brand Scaling Funnel

Use this workflow for Gum Cars Window Tinting, with commercial and small-business accounts as the primary growth lane:

1. Clarify the commercial offer, service area, proof assets, capacity, and ideal account types.
2. Build awareness with proof-led local content and account-based outreach to owners and property decision makers.
3. Route every piece to one commercial assessment or quote action.
4. Respond quickly with building type, window scope, business priority, timing, and decision-maker questions.
5. Recommend a site visit or measurement step, then send a clear proposal and book the install.
6. Follow up with scheduling reminders and a post-install review or referral request.
7. Measure target accounts, conversations, assessments, proposals, close rate, revenue, response time, and reviews.

Start with the smallest test that can produce learning in seven days. Do not claim performance before data exists.
